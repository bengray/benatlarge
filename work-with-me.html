02

Shelters

Free, for shelters and rescues in DuPage County.

A dog photographed badly sits in a kennel longer. I will come to you, work around your intake schedule, and hand back files you can use on your own listings, your socials, and your print material with no credit and no conditions. There is no catch and no upsell.

One thing to be straight about: most of my week now goes to the twelve-month project, so outside that I can take on roughly one shelter day a month. I keep a list and work down it in order. If you need pictures for a specific dog urgently, say so in the email and that jumps the list.
