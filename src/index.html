Not breed standard.
Not a gray backdrop.
Not sit, stay, good boy.

Some photographers want to make your pet look like a greeting card. I’m not one of them. I photograph pets as the absolute legends they are: intense, ridiculous, a little feral, fully themselves. Your pet has a story to tell. I want to tell it.
